## Key Philosophy: The Honest Path

The widget works because it tells the **truth**:

- **Accurate profile:** Technical Operations Manager (not "full stack engineer")
- **Real achievements:** 72→5h automation, ACIL on Marketplace, led 0→15+ team
- **Clear education:** College coursework + UNLV/Institute of Data cert (2025)
- **Honest boundaries:** "I don't discuss architecture for security reasons"
- **Plain English:** No jargon, no obfuscation, no honeypot traps
- **Open to work:** Clear signal on every page

**Why this wins:** Recruiters trust people who are straight. Jargon kills trust.

## FAQs

### Q: Is this really zero cost right now?
**A:** Yes. Ollama runs locally on your machine. Railway free tier. No APIs. But understand that's Phase 0-2 only. Phase 3+ will need basic infrastructure.

### Q: Can I still get a job while doing Phase 1?
**A:** Absolutely. In fact, the widget IS your job search tool. Share it with recruiters as your portfolio.

### Q: What if I want to accelerate?
**A:** You can, but it's not recommended. Phase 1 validation is critical. If you skip it and launch broken, you'll lose trust. Better to slow-cook it right than fast-fail it wrong.

### Q: What if Phase 1 feedback is bad?
**A:** See Phase-1-Validation.md → "Failure Modes & Recovery". Most issues (robotic tone, generic responses, etc) are fixable in Phase 2.

### Q: Do I need to be technical to do this?
**A:** No. You manage the recruiter outreach, collect feedback, and make decisions. The code is already deployed. Your job is the business side.

### Q: Can I modify FT0's personality?
**A:** Yes, but not during Phase 1. Keep it stable, collect data, then iterate in Phase 2. That's the plan.

# LinkedIn Recruiter Search Cheat Sheet

## Quick Start (Copy & Paste These Searches)

Go to **linkedin.com** → Click **Search** → Choose **People**

---

## SEARCH 1: Technical Recruiters (Biggest Pool)

**Paste this in search bar:**
```
"Technical Recruiter" location:"United States" role:"Recruiting"
```

**OR try these variants:**
```
"Technical Recruiter" OR "Tech Recruiter" location:"United States"
"Engineering Recruiter" location:"United States"
"Talent Acquisition" OR "Talent Acquisition Manager" location:"United States"
```

**Filter by:**
- Location: Your city or "Remote"
- Company size: 50-500 people (most responsive)
- Industry: Software, Technology, Staffing (Agencies)

**Expected:** 50-200+ results
**Pick:** Top 5 most relevant (look for recent activity)

---

## SEARCH 2: Engineering Managers (Growing Companies)

**Paste this:**
```
"Engineering Manager" OR "VP Engineering" location:"United States" 
company:("Series A" OR "Series B" OR "hiring")
```

**OR:**
```
"VP Engineering" OR "Head of Engineering" location:"United States" 
current:"hiring"
```

**Filter by:**
- Company size: 50-300 (sweet spot for early adopter culture)
- Recent posts (they mention hiring)

**Expected:** 30-100 results
**Pick:** Top 2-3 (prioritize those with recent hiring posts)

---

## SEARCH 3: Recruiting Agency Owners/Managers

**Paste this:**
```
"Founder" OR "Partner" OR "Director" 
company:("Recruiting" OR "Staffing" OR "Talent" OR "Executive Search")
location:"United States"
```

**OR:**
```
"Recruiting Manager" OR "Staffing Manager" 
industry:"Staffing and Recruiting"
location:"United States"
```

**Filter by:**
- Company size: 5-50 people (boutique firms are most responsive)
- Recent activity

**Expected:** 20-50 results
**Pick:** Top 1-2 (focus on boutique, not mega-firms)

---

## SEARCH 4: Target Company Hiring Managers (Specific)

**For each company you want to work for:**

```
company:"[Company Name]" 
(title:"Hiring Manager" OR "VP" OR "Head of" OR "People Operations")
```

**Example:**
```
company:"Google" title:"VP" location:"United States"
```

**Filter by:**
- Department: Engineering, Operations, Product
- Most recent activity (they're actively hiring)

**Expected:** 5-20 results per company
**Pick:** 1 per company (prioritize VP/Head level)

## Pro Tips for LinkedIn Search

### Tip 1: Use Quotes for Exact Phrases
```
"Technical Recruiter" finds exact phrase
Technical Recruiter finds any order
```

### Tip 2: Use OR for Variations
```
"Tech Recruiter" OR "Technical Recruiter" OR "Engineering Recruiter"
```

### Tip 3: Filter AFTER Search
- Search first (gets broad results)
- Then apply filters on left sidebar
- Easier than building perfect search string

### Tip 4: Sort by Relevance
- Results sorted by "Most relevant" by default
- Best candidates at top

### Tip 4: Look at Recent Posts
- Click on person
- Scroll to their recent posts/activity
- If they posted about hiring → they're hiring
- Recent activity = more likely to respond

## Personal Network Search (Source 4)

**Don't search LinkedIn for this. Think manually:**

- Former colleagues in HR/Recruiting
- Friends who've moved to hiring roles
- Connections in startup communities
- Anyone who's ever recruited you
- People you've worked with

**How to contact:**
- Email directly (if you know them)
- LinkedIn message (if you're connected)
- Mutual friend intro (best)

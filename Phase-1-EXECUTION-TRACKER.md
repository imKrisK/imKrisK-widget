## STEP 1: Build Your Recruiter List (2 Hours)

### The 4 Sources Breakdown

```
TARGET SPLIT:
- 4-5 LinkedIn recruiters (biggest pool, cold outreach)
- 2-3 Startup hiring managers (warm culture fit)
- 1-2 Recruiting agencies (specialized, high signal)
- 1-2 Personal network (warmest, highest response rate)
= 10 total recruiters
```

### SOURCE 1: LinkedIn Recruiter Search (Easiest - Start Here)

**How to search:**
1. Go to linkedin.com
2. Click "Search" → Jobs or People (use People)
3. In search box, type one of these:
   ```
   "Technical Recruiter" AND "Hiring"
   "Engineering Manager" AND "Recruiting"
   "Talent Acquisition" AND "Tech"
   "HR Manager" AND "Technology"
   ```

4. Filter by location (your city or "Remote")
5. Filter by company size (50-500 people = early adopters)

**Copy 4-5 names with links to your tracker below**

**Example:**
- Jane Smith | Technical Recruiter | TechStaff Solutions | linkedin.com/in/janesmith
- Bob Chen | Engineering Manager | StartupXYZ | linkedin.com/in/bobchen

---

### SOURCE 2: Startup Hiring Managers (Warm Culture)

**How to find:**
1. Think of 3-5 startups you'd want to work for
2. Go to their LinkedIn company page
3. Click "See all employees" → search for:
   - "VP Engineering"
   - "Hiring Manager"
   - "People Ops"
   - "Head of HR"

4. Look at their recent posts (they mention hiring)

**Copy 2-3 names with links to tracker**

**Example:**
- Alice Rodriguez | VP Engineering | Cool Startup Inc | linkedin.com/in/alicerodriguez
- David Park | Hiring Manager | TechCorp | linkedin.com/in/davidpark

---

### SOURCE 3: Recruiting Agencies (Specialized)

**How to find:**
1. Google search: "tech recruiting agencies [your city]"
2. Or: "executive recruiting [your industry]"
3. Look for 5-50 person firms (nimble, boutique)
4. Find founder/partner contact
5. LinkedIn them

**Copy 1-2 names with links**

**Example:**
- Sarah Johnson | Founder | TechTalent Partners | linkedin.com/in/sarahjohnson

---

### SOURCE 4: Personal Network (Warmest)

**Think of:**
- Former colleagues in HR/recruiting
- Friends working at target companies
- Connections in startup communities
- People you've worked with who moved to HR
- Anyone who's ever recruited you before

**Why warmest:** Personal intro = 80%+ response rate

**Copy 1-2 names**

**Example:**
- Mike Davis | Friend from StartupX, now HR Manager at BigCo | Know via email
- Lisa Chen | Former colleague, now VP People at Scale | LinkedIn connection

## STEP 2: Create Google Form Feedback Survey (1 Hour)

### Form Setup (Copy & Paste)

1. Go to **forms.google.com**
2. Click **+ Create new form**
3. Title: **"imKrisK Widget - Recruiter Feedback"**
4. Add these questions:

---

### Question 1: Tone
**Title:** How natural does the conversation feel?
**Type:** Multiple choice (Linear scale)
**Scale:** 1-10
**Labels:**
- 1 = "Robotic AI"
- 10 = "Real Person"

---

### Question 2: Quality
**Title:** How impressive is the response quality?
**Type:** Multiple choice (Linear scale)
**Scale:** 1-10
**Labels:**
- 1 = "Generic / Vague"
- 10 = "Impressive / Specific"

---

### Question 3: Hiring Decision
**Title:** Would you move this person forward in hiring?
**Type:** Multiple choice
**Options:**
- [ ] Yes
- [ ] No
- [ ] Not sure

---

### Question 4: Open Feedback
**Title:** Any specific feedback or suggestions?
**Type:** Short answer or Paragraph
**Required:** No

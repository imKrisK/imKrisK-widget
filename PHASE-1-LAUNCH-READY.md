## ❓ COMMON QUESTIONS

**Q: What if I don't get 5 responses?**  
A: That's OK. 3 high-quality responses > 5 low-quality. Quality > Quantity.

**Q: What if feedback is low (5/10)?**  
A: Then Phase 2 will focus on what to fix. No shame. Data wins.

**Q: Can I send more than 5 in Week 1?**  
A: Sure, but 5 is manageable. Sending 10 all at once may lower quality. Better to send 5, monitor, learn, then send 5 more.

**Q: Should I mention it's AI?**  
A: Yes, be transparent. FT0 is AI. That's the whole story. Recruiters respect honesty.

**Q: What if someone asks for a resume?**  
A: Give it to them! The widget is a conversation starter, not a resume replacement.

**Q: Can I change the prompts mid-Phase 1?**  
A: Not recommended. Keep it stable for 4 weeks, collect data, then iterate in Phase 2.

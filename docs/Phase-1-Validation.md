# Phase 1: Production Validation (Weeks 1-2 | 4 Weeks Total)

**Goal:** Get 5+ recruiters to try the widget, collect honest feedback, validate it's impressive.

**Success Criteria:**
- ✅ 10 recruiter outreaches sent
- ✅ 3-5 responses (30-50% conversion)
- ✅ Average feedback: 6.5+/10
- ✅ "Would hire" sentiment: 60%+
- ✅ Clear patterns identified

**Effort:** 5 hrs/week

## Recruiter Sources & Search Queries

### SOURCE 1: LinkedIn Recruiter Search
**Search string:**
```
"Technical Recruiter" OR "Engineering Manager" OR "Talent Acquisition"
AND (location: your_city OR remote)
```

**Best targets:**
- 3rd-party recruiting firms (they love seeing candidates)
- In-house tech company recruiters
- Startup founders who hire

**Message template:**
```
Hi [Name],

I built an AI widget that tells my professional story. 
Since you see tons of candidates, would you try it and 
give me quick feedback on whether it feels authentic?

[widget_url]

Appreciate it!
[Your name]
```

### SOURCE 2: Startup Hiring Managers
**Search method:**
- Go to LinkedIn
- Find startups you'd want to work for
- Look for "Hiring Manager", "VP Engineering", "CTO"
- Check their recent posts about hiring

**Best targets:**
- Fast-growing Series A/B startups
- 50-200 person teams (early adopter culture)
- Tech-forward companies

**Message template:**
```
Hi [Name],

I know you're hiring. I built something to showcase 
my work to hiring managers like you. Would love your 
30-second feedback on whether it's effective.

[widget_url]

Thanks!
[Your name]
```

### SOURCE 3: Recruiting Agencies
**Search method:**
- Google: "tech recruiting firms [your city]"
- LinkedIn: filter for Recruiting companies
- Look for 5-20 person agencies (nimble)

**Best targets:**
- Specialized tech recruiting (not generalist)
- Executive search firms for ops/management
- Boutique firms that know your industry

**Message template:**
```
Hi [Name],

Building a new way for ops/tech candidates to market 
themselves. Thought you might find this useful or find 
it interesting. Would appreciate feedback.

[widget_url]

Thanks!
[Your name]
```

### SOURCE 4: Personal Network
**Think of:**
- LinkedIn connections who are recruiters
- Former colleagues in HR/recruiting
- Friends at target companies
- Slack communities (founders, startup folks)

**Best targets:**
- Anyone you've worked with (warm)

## What to Watch For

As recruiters use the widget, **watch for patterns:**

### ✅ Signs It's Working
- Feedback score 7+/10
- Comments like "feels authentic" or "impressive"
- Questions about specific achievements (72→5h, ACIL)
- Follow-up message: "When can we talk?"
- Quick response time (within 1-2 days)

### ⚠️ Signs Something's Off
- Feedback score <6/10
- Comments like "feels generic" or "needs personality"
- Questions about infrastructure (means boundary detector needs work)
- No follow-up ("Interesting but...")
- Slow response time

### 🔴 Red Flags
- Many people say "tone feels robotic"
- "Would this person actually code?"
- Infrastructure questions not being blocked
- Chat errors or crashes
- Conversations dying mid-way

---

## What to Do with Feedback

### If feedback is ✅ Positive (7+/10)
**Decision:** Proceed to Phase 2
- Optimize response quality based on specific feedback
- Focus on what's working (keep it)
- Fix edge cases discovered

### If feedback is ⚠️ Mixed (5-7/10)
**Decision:** Extra iteration + Phase 2
- Something's partially working
- Need one more recruiter round with tweaks
- Phase 2 should focus on what's missing

### If feedback is 🔴 Poor (<5/10)
**Decision:** Pause Phase 2, investigate
- Possible root cause: tone, response quality, or UX
- Ask follow-up questions to understand why
- May need to revisit system prompt or Ollama model

---

## Failure Modes & Recovery

### Issue: "Conversations feel robotic"
**Fix:** Phase 2 → personality adjustment in FT0 prompt
**Timeline:** 1 week

### Issue: "Infrastructure questions don't get honest answers"
**Fix:** Check architecture-boundary.ts is catching them
**Timeline:** 1 day

### Issue: "Responses are too generic"
**Fix:** Test different Ollama models (Phase 2)
**Timeline:** 2-3 days per model

### Issue: "No responses from recruiters"
**Fix:** Improve message (test different versions)
**Timeline:** 3 days, send batch 2 with new message

### Issue: "Widget crashes or times out"
**Fix:** Check Railway logs, restart service
**Timeline:** 1 day

## Questions? Common Asks

**Q: What if a recruiter asks to skip the widget and just get my resume?**
A: Give it to them. The widget isn't a barrier; it's a conversation starter.

**Q: Should I mention I built it with AI?**
A: It's transparent on the site (FT0 = AI assistant). Be honest if asked.

**Q: Can I iterate the prompt mid-Phase 1?**
A: Not recommended. Keep it stable, collect data, iterate in Phase 2.

**Q: How long should they spend on the widget?**
A: Target 2-5 minutes. If <30 sec, they didn't engage.

**Q: What if they ask technical details about the stack?**
A: Say "I don't discuss architecture for security reasons" (FT0 is trained on this).
